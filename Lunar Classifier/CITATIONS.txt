DATA CITATIONS
Lunar South Pole Landing Site Classifier
===============================================================================

1. TOPOLOGY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Data:       5 m/pixel LOLA Digital Elevation Models for south pole landing
            sites (elevation, slope, slope error, roughness, total error) -
            Site01 (Connecting Ridge), Site04 (Shackleton rim), Site23
            (Malapert Massif).
Repository: NASA Goddard Planetary Geodesy Dynamics Archive (PGDA)
            https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/LOLA_5mpp/
Source:     NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter
            (LRO) / Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter (LOLA)

Cite as:
Barker, M. K., Mazarico, E., Neumann, G. A., Smith, D. E., Zuber, M. T., &
Head, J. W. (2021). Improved LOLA elevation maps for south pole landing
sites: Error estimates and their impact on illumination conditions.
Planetary and Space Science, 203, 105119.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pss.2020.105119

Companion instrument citation:
Smith, D. E., Zuber, M. T., Jackson, G. B., et al. (2010). The Lunar
Orbiter Laser Altimeter Investigation on the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter
Mission. Space Science Reviews, 150, 209-241.
https://doi.org/10.1007/s11214-009-9512-y


2. COMMUNICATIONS (solar illumination + Earth visibility) AND WATER/ICE
   PROXY (permanent shadow fraction)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Data:       Lunar Polar Illumination products - average solar illumination
            (AVGVISIB), average Earth visibility (AVGVISIB_EARTH), and
            permanently shadowed regions (LPSR). 85S-90S, 60 m/pixel.
Repository: NASA Goddard Planetary Geodesy Dynamics Archive (PGDA)
            https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/69
Source:     NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / LRO / LOLA

Cite as (per the dataset's own citation request):
Mazarico, E., Neumann, G. A., Smith, D. E., Zuber, M. T., & Torrence, M. H.
(2011). Illumination conditions of the lunar polar regions using LOLA
topography. Icarus, 211(2), 1066-1081.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2010.10.030


3. POINTS OF INTEREST (named craters, massifs, etc.)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Data:       Named lunar surface features near each site (e.g. Mons Malapert,
            Lhamu, Shackleton Crater).
Repository: Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature
            https://planetarynames.wr.usgs.gov/
Source:     USGS Astrogeology Science Center, maintained on behalf of the
            IAU Working Group for Planetary System Nomenclature.
            NOTE: this is the one dataset in this project that is not
            NASA-hosted - it is public-domain US federal government data
            (USGS), which is the standard reference NASA itself uses for
            lunar nomenclature.

Cite as:
International Astronomical Union (IAU) Working Group for Planetary System
Nomenclature. Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature. USGS Astrogeology
Science Center. Retrieved 2026-11-13, from
https://planetarynames.wr.usgs.gov/
