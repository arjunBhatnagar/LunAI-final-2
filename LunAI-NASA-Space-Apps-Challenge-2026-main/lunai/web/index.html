What can I upload?

Trajectories: columns t (s) and rx ry rz (m, Earth-centred inertial). Optional: vx vy vz, moon_rx moon_ry moon_rz, target_rx target_ry target_rz, mass, thrust_N, stage_index, altitude, target_distance, mdot, ax ay az, phase, utc, sun_rx sun_ry sun_rz. Shown at true scale with Earth fixed at the centre.

Result tables: any CSV with latitude and longitude columns (lat/lon or lat_deg/lon_deg), such as the classifier's Site*_candidates.csv. Each row becomes a spot on the Moon. A composite_score or score column is shown as the score; all other columns are listed as they are.
